Who we are

Solar energy without extra promises

Kehayov Solar designs, supplies and installs photovoltaic systems for homes and businesses. We are based in Rudozem — and work with clients across Bulgaria.

For more than 10 years we have built systems with JinkoSolar panels and Deye inverters and batteries. Over 50 completed sites — no universal template for every roof.
