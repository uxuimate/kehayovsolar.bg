Package description

The 6.12 kWp hybrid package suits households with moderate consumption that want on-site generation and essential storage. It combines 12 JinkoSolar 510 W panels, a Deye 6 kW single-phase LV hybrid inverter and a Deye 11.8 kWh battery.

Mounting structure and installation are included in the indicative price. Exact scope is confirmed after a site visit — roof, load and goals — not a one-size catalogue template.

Kit

  • Battery Deye 11.8 kWh
