Package description

The 20.4 kWp package is a three-phase solution for large homes and business sites with higher load. It includes 40 JinkoSolar 510 W panels, a Deye 20 kW three-phase LV hybrid inverter and two Deye 16 kWh batteries (32 kWh total).

The price is indicative and includes structure and installation. For three-phase sites a visit is essential — to size capacity, protection and grid connection correctly.

Kit

What is included

  • Solar panels 40 × 510 W JinkoSolar
  • Hybrid inverter Deye 20 kW, three-phase LV
  • Batteries Deye 2 × 16 kWh
  • Roof mounting structure
  • Solar cabling and connectors
  • AC/DC protection and installation materials
  • Equipment delivery
  • Professional installation and commissioning
