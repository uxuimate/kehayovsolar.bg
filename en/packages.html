Roof or ground

There is no universally better mounting — it depends on the site. A roof is the natural choice when orientation and free area are suitable. Ground-mount is discussed when the roof is shaded, complex in shape, or too small for the needed capacity. Panel count follows consumption and sizing after a site visit — not a catalogue template.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does the package cost?

Price is on request. It depends on exact capacity, the roof, the inverter and whether there is a battery. We quote after a site visit.

Which package do I need — 5, 10 or 20 kW?

There is no single answer. It depends on consumption, available area and your goals. Packages are a guide — configuration is confirmed after a consultation.

Is a battery included?

Not by default. A Deye battery is optional when it makes sense for the site.

Is a building permit needed?

For systems up to 20 kW on existing residential buildings a simplified Spatial Development Act procedure often applies. The specific case is checked for your property.

Ready to generate your own energy?

Tell us about your property and your energy needs. We will discuss the right solution for you.
