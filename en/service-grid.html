Grid-tied photovoltaic systems

A system connected to the distribution grid — you generate energy by day and use the grid when you need it.

Request a quote
what it is

What is a grid-tied system?

A grid-tied photovoltaic system is connected to the distribution grid. The panels generate energy by day; when generation is short — in the evening or in cloudy weather — the site uses grid power.

A battery is not required. This is the most direct option when you already have a supply and want your own generation without separate storage at this stage.

suitable for

Who this system is for

A grid-tied system suits a site that already has a supply and wants its own generation without a required battery. By day you use energy from the panels; when output is short, power comes from the grid.

  • Homes with a grid connection
  • Offices, commercial and production sites
  • Sites with daytime consumption
  • Clients who want a system without a battery at this stage

composition

What usually goes into the system

The exact kit is confirmed after a site visit. The project usually includes:

  • JinkoSolar panels and a roof or ground structure
  • An inverter that converts power for use on site
  • Cabling, protection and connection to the internal installation
  • Design, supply and installation

A battery is added only when it makes sense — then we talk about a hybrid configuration or a separate storage system.

storage

Can the energy be stored?

Yes — if a battery is added to the system. The grid-tied installation itself works without one: by day you use the generation, when it is short — the grid.

Storage makes sense when you use power outside sunny hours or want more of your own energy. We use Deye battery systems when they suit the site.

Roof or ground. How many panels are needed.

There is no universally better mounting — it depends on the site. A roof is the natural choice when orientation and free area are suitable. Ground-mount is discussed when the roof is shaded, complex or too small for the required capacity.

Panel count follows consumption, available area, solar orientation and shading — not a catalogue template. We size the system after a site visit.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is a battery required?

No. A grid-tied system works without a battery. By day you use energy from the panels; when output is short, power comes from the grid.

Does the system work in cloudy weather?

Yes, there is generation in cloudy weather, though less. At night or when output is short you use grid power.

How is capacity determined?

According to consumption, the roof and the site’s goals. The configuration is confirmed after a site visit — there is no single solution for every property.

How much does a grid-tied system cost?

The price depends on capacity, the roof and the equipment. We quote after an enquiry and a site visit — no ready-made prices on the site.
