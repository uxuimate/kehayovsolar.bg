Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does installation take?

It depends on the site — capacity, roof or ground, access and connection readiness. After a site visit we can give a more realistic timeline for your case.

Do you work across Bulgaria?

Yes. We are based in Rudozem and work with clients across Bulgaria.

Can we visit a reference site?

After an enquiry we discuss whether a suitable nearby site exists and under what conditions a visit is possible. We do not run a public site-visit programme.

Ready to generate your own energy?

Tell us about your property and your energy needs. We will discuss the right solution for you.
