Package description

A balanced hybrid system for family homes with higher consumption and a greater need for energy storage.

The technical setup includes 12 × 590 W JinkoSolar panels, a Deye 8 kW single-phase hybrid inverter and a 15 kWh battery. Mounting and installation are included. The exact setup is confirmed after a site visit.
