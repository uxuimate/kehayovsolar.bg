Privacy policy

Information under Art. 13 GDPR and the Personal Data Protection Act — what we process, why, and what your rights are.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This policy describes how Kehayov Solar processes personal data you give us through the website or when you contact us directly. It follows Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Bulgarian Personal Data Protection Act.

1. Controller

The data controller is Kehayov Solar, based in Rudozem, phone +359 87 848 9013, email hiki7787@gmail.com.

If the full legal name and UIC become available, they will be added here after the client provides them.

2. What data we collect

When you enquire via the contact form, email or phone we may process: name, phone, email, locality, topic, property type, grid connection, preferred mounting (roof/ground), seasonality, a description of what you want to power, and the message text. When you visit the site, the browser may send standard technical data (IP address, device type) needed to display the page.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process data to answer an enquiry or prepare a quote/consultation (pre-contractual steps at your request). Data needed for accounting after a contract is processed on a legal-obligation basis. We do not send marketing newsletters unless you have expressly asked for that communication.

4. Retention and sharing

We keep correspondence as long as needed to reply, deliver the service and meet applicable record-keeping periods. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose it only if the law requires it.

5. Cookies

The site uses technical scripts needed for the pages to work. We do not use advertising pixels or third-party analytics cookies unless they are added later with separate notice.

6. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability under the GDPR. To exercise them write to hiki7787@gmail.com. You may lodge a complaint with the Commission for Personal Data Protection.
