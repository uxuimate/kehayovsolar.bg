Hybrid photovoltaic systems

Solar energy, a grid connection and optional storage — when a battery suits the site.

Request a quote

what it is

What is a hybrid system?

A hybrid system is connected to the grid and can include a battery. You generate energy by day, use it on site, and surplus can be stored — when a battery is part of the project.

The difference from a purely grid-tied installation is control of your own generation outside sunny hours. We use Deye inverters and batteries when that is appropriate.

Suitable for

Who this system is for

  • Households that want more control of their energy
  • Sites with evening or after-sunset consumption
  • Clients planning a battery now or at a later stage
  • Business sites with uneven load

What usually goes into the system

The exact kit is confirmed after a site visit. The project usually includes:

  • Design
  • Roof or ground mounting structure
  • JinkoSolar panels
  • An inverter that converts power for use on site
  • A battery is added only when it makes sense — then we talk about a hybrid configuration or a separate storage system.
  • Cabling, protection and connection to the internal installation
  • Supply and installation
storage

A battery now or later

A hybrid inverter lets the system work with the grid and accept a battery when it makes sense. Storage does not have to be installed on day one.

We discuss it for evening use, uneven load or when you want more of your own energy. Capacity is chosen after a site visit.

Roof or ground. How many panels are needed.

There is no universally better mounting — it depends on the site. A roof is the natural choice when orientation and free area are suitable. Ground-mount is discussed when the roof is shaded, complex or too small for the required capacity.

Panel count follows consumption, available area and whether a battery is planned now or later. The hybrid configuration is confirmed after a site visit.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference from a grid-tied system?

A hybrid system is also connected to the grid, but it can include a battery. Part of the energy is used immediately and surplus can be stored when a battery suits the site.

Is a battery required immediately?

No. We use Deye inverters and batteries when it makes sense. A battery can be planned now or later — confirmed during design.

Is it suitable for evening consumption?

A hybrid configuration with a battery is discussed when there is consumption outside sunny hours or when you want more control of the energy you produce.

How much does a hybrid system cost?

It depends on capacity, whether there is a battery and the specifics of the site. The quote follows an enquiry and a site visit.
