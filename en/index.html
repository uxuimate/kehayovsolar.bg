Choosing

How we choose the system

We do not start with a catalogue capacity. First we clarify who the site is for and what type of system it needs — then a site visit, sizing and a quote.

01

Step 1

Who it is for

Home For the home Household use, the roof and your goals — the basis for sizing. Business For business Company sites, commercial space and production — matched to load and operating hours.
02

Step 2

System type

Grid-tied Your own generation with a grid connection — no battery required. → Hybrid Panels and a Deye inverter, with a battery option now or later. → Off-grid When the grid is missing or independence is a real priority. → Storage (BESS) A Deye battery — when the site’s operating profile justifies it. →

Roof or ground

There is no universally better mounting — it depends on the site. A roof is the natural choice when orientation and free area are suitable. Ground-mount is discussed when the roof is shaded, complex or too small for the required capacity.

After these two steps comes the site visit — and only then capacity, configuration and the ready packages below.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a photovoltaic system cost?

The price depends on the required capacity, the panels and inverter, whether there is a battery, the roof, and the grid connection. After a site visit and a review of your consumption we can propose a solution for that property.

What capacity suits my property?

There is no single answer for every property. Capacity depends mainly on electricity use, the consumption profile, available area and site specifics. We can propose a suitable configuration after a consultation.

Do I need a battery?

Not in every case. A battery lets you store part of the electricity produced and use it later. The choice depends on site consumption, how the system will run, and how much independence you want. We use Deye battery systems when they suit the property.

Is a building permit required?

It depends on the system and the property. For existing single-family homes and holiday houses, the Spatial Development Act provides a relief: renewable installations for own consumption with total installed capacity up to 20 kW do not require a building permit when the statutory conditions are met. Other cases may need a building permit and/or other procedures. Each property should be checked in advance.

How is the system connected to the electricity grid?

The procedure depends on the site, the capacity and how the generated electricity will be used. We will walk you through the steps for that property.

Can the system be installed on my roof?

Not every roof is equally suitable. Design considers structure and condition, orientation, pitch, shading, available area and mounting method. That is why a site visit and a preliminary assessment are an important part of the process.

Do you work across Bulgaria?

Yes. We work with clients across Bulgaria.

What happens after installation?

After installation the system is checked and the steps needed to start it and run it normally are completed, according to the project and applicable requirements.
